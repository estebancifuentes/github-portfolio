v1.4.0 Complete feat(remote-sensing): coastal marsh land-cover classification

NASA Coastal Marsh Change Study

WFU Engineering — Di Vittorio Lab · Research Assistant
Context
A NASA-funded study needed a scalable way to track coastal marsh change over time across dozens of watersheds — too much ground-truth data to collect manually.
My role
Built automated multi-temporal land-cover classification workflows in Google Earth Engine, prepared and validated geospatial training data in ArcGIS Pro, and evaluated model accuracy in MATLAB. Mentored an undergraduate intern on the same methods.
Outcome
Delivered a calibrated, scalable prediction model validated across 57 watersheds, and presented the findings at the WFU Undergraduate Research Symposium.
Industry Context
Environmental and sustainability metrics are moving from research-only datasets into standard business reporting, which is raising demand for analysts who can work with geospatial and remote-sensing data at this level of rigor.
ToolsGoogle Earth Engine · ArcGIS Pro · MATLAB ApproachManual ground-truth sampling → automated multi-temporal classification
v1.0.0 Complete feat(gis): community planning decision-support tool

Community Planning GIS Application

Independent Analytical Project
Context
Parcel, zoning, demographic, and infrastructure data for Winston-Salem existed across separate public sources, making it hard for local planners to identify where community initiatives would have the most impact.
My role
Integrated the four datasets into a single interactive GIS application in ArcGIS Pro and applied spatial analysis to surface suitable locations.
Outcome
Shipped a decision-support tool that consolidates previously scattered public datasets into one place for local planning decisions.
Industry Context
Local governments and planning agencies are increasingly investing in centralized geospatial platforms to replace fragmented public data systems — this project anticipated that exact shift on a local scale.
ToolsArcGIS Pro · ArcGIS Online ApproachScattered public datasets → single spatial decision-support tool
