Market lens

Why this bridge matters right now.

Three shifts I'm tracking as I move from environmental research into business analytics.

01

ESG data is now a reporting requirement, not a research niche

Environmental risk and sustainability metrics are moving into standard financial disclosures, which means companies need analysts who can handle geospatial and environmental datasets with the same rigor as sales or operations data.

02

Remote sensing is going commercial

Satellite and aerial data that used to live only in research labs is now priced into insurance underwriting, agriculture, and infrastructure planning — a direct market for someone who has already built these pipelines in an academic setting.

03

The scarce skill is translation, not tooling

Most technical graduates can run a model. Fewer can explain what the output means for a budget, a permit, or a client decision — which is the specific gap this hybrid background is built to close.
